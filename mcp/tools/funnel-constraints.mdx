## diagnose_funnel

Scope `constraints:read` — you can grant this on a key you create yourself.

START HERE for any 'why isn't this client's paid media working' question. Follows the paid funnel (impressions → clicks → sessions → leads → conversions) and returns a ranked list of what to fix, each with what it means and what to do about it. Self-configuring: with no conversion_event it inventories the client's events and picks the best pairing, reporting which it used. CRITICALLY, it trust-gates on tracking — when too few ad clicks resolve to a synced campaign it says so and attaches the tracking audit instead of handing back a funnel that is really measuring UTM hygiene. Read `brief.headline` first, then `brief.findings` in order (they are ranked by recoverable conversions, NOT by worst percentage — a 5× shortfall on 1,000 impressions is a rounding error). `brief.scale` lists rows with nothing to fix: budget candidates, not optimisation candidates.

## audit_funnel_tracking

Scope `constraints:read` — you can grant this on a key you create yourself.

Tracking hygiene per campaign: which campaigns are measurable at all, and where the untracked ad clicks went. Use this whenever diagnose_funnel reports the tracking step or a low match rate. Lead with `totals.flagged_spend` — that is the spend whose effect cannot be measured. Verdicts worst-first: no_sessions, low_tracking, name_only (matching on campaign name, breaks silently at the next rename), no_clicks (no delivery — NOT a fault), ok. Untracked clicks are bucketed by cause because each is a different repair: no_utm, unexpanded_macro, unknown_campaign. NOTE: no_sessions is not automatically a bug — an on-platform objective (lead form, messaging) never sends anyone to the site, so zero sessions is the correct reading for it. Check the objective before hunting for a missing pixel. Meta only. No volume floor, unlike the analysis.

## list_funnel_events

Scope `constraints:read` — you can grant this on a key you create yourself.

Which of this client's events can serve as the conversion or lead step, with volume, session coverage, person coverage, and which JSON paths carry money. Call this when diagnose_funnel couldn't pick an event, or to override its choice. A session-less event is NOT unusable — CRM/webhook conversions (GoHighLevel, Stripe) never touch the browser and are credited through the person instead; `usable:false` means neither a session nor a person, so nothing can attach it to a campaign. A lead_event is held to a stricter standard: the landing step is measured per session, so a session-less lead event would report a landing rate of zero and blame the page.

## analyze_funnel

Scope `constraints:read` — you can grant this on a key you create yourself.

The raw Constraint Finder response, with every knob exposed and no trust gate, ranking, or auto-configuration applied. Prefer diagnose_funnel — it wraps this and adds the parts that stop the output being misread. Reach for this only when you need the untransformed rows or a per-step benchmark override for a single what-if. ALWAYS read `tracking.match_rate` before anything else in the response.

## get_funnel_benchmarks

Scope `constraints:read` — you can grant this on a key you create yourself.

The standing benchmark targets stored for this client — account-wide defaults and any per-campaign overrides, with the note recorded against each. Without a stored target a step is judged against the account's own pooled rate, so these are what turn 'below your own average' into 'below what we promised'.

## set_funnel_benchmark

Scope `constraints:write` — your agency grants this one.

Store a standing target the funnel is judged against, account-wide or for one campaign. Rates, not percentages: a 2% CTR is 0.02, and anything above 1 is rejected. THIS REPLACES THE WHOLE SCOPE — any step you omit falls back to the account's pooled rate rather than keeping its previous stored value, so always send the complete set for that scope. Prefer a campaign id over a name: a name-matched target silently stops matching the next time the campaign is renamed.

## clear_funnel_benchmark

Scope `constraints:write` — your agency grants this one.

Remove stored benchmark targets, returning those steps to the account's own pooled rate. Pass `step` to clear one step across scopes, `campaign` to clear that campaign's scope, or neither to clear the account-wide default.
