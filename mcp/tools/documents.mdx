## search_knowledge

Scope `documents:read` — you can grant this on a key you create yourself.

Semantic + keyword search across this client's knowledge documents, discussion threads, and ingested Slack conversations. Finds relevant passages by meaning, not just exact words. Returns ranked excerpts; follow up with get_document(source_id) for full document text or get_discussions for full threads.
