## create_glossary_term

Scope `glossary:write` — your agency grants this one.

Create a new client-specific glossary term. This term will only be visible for this client.

## update_glossary_term

Scope `glossary:write` — your agency grants this one.

Update an existing client-specific glossary term. Can only update terms owned by this client.

## delete_glossary_term

Scope `glossary:write` — your agency grants this one.

Delete a client-specific glossary term. Can only delete terms owned by this client.

## set_glossary_override

Scope `glossary:write` — your agency grants this one.

Override a global glossary term's definition for this client. The global term remains unchanged for other clients.

## remove_glossary_override

Scope `glossary:write` — your agency grants this one.

Remove a client's override for a global glossary term, reverting to the global definition.
