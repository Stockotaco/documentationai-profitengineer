## Overview

Profit Engineer lets you turn your marketing data into clear next steps.

## Getting Started

1. Log in to your Mythic OS dashboard
2. Connect your ad accounts (Google, Meta, etc.)
3. Select a campaign to analyze
4. Click the “Engineer Profit” button

Profit Engineer will analyze campaigns and provide recommended actions.
