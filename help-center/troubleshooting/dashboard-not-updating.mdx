---
title: Dashboard Not Updating
description: How to resolve display updates in Profit Engineer.
---

## Symptoms
- Campaign data looks outdated
- Numbers don't reflect recent spend

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Refresh Data">
    Click the "Sync Data" button at the top right of your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Integrations">
    Ensure all ad account connections are active under Settings > Integrations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear Cache">
    Log out and log back in.
  </Step>
</Steps>
