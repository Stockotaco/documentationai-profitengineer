## Analyze ROI in Minutes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profit Engineer">
    From your Mythic OS dashboard, select Profit Engineer under Tools.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Campaign">
    Choose a campaign from the list to view data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Insights">
    The tool displays ROI, cost per conversion, and suggests improvements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download Report">
    Click "Export PDF" for a downloadable summary.
  </Step>
</Steps>
