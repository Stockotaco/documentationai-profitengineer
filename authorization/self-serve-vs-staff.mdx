---
title: "Self-serve vs agency-granted"
description: "Which permissions you can grant yourself, and which your agency has to add."
---

You can create keys for yourself without waiting on anyone. What you can't do is grant
yourself every permission — some writes are held back, and this page says which and why.

## What you can grant yourself

- **Every read scope.** Anything that only reads your own workspace.
- **Four writes**, all of which act on your own knowledge and are reversible:

| Scope | What it writes |
|-------|---------------|
| `memories:write` | Knowledge the AI keeps between sessions |
| `documents:write` | Your knowledge-base documents |
| `discussions:write` | Comments and replies on your reports |
| `tasks:write` | Scheduled task definitions |

`tasks:write` is on this list because a task is inert on its own. Creating one changes
nothing until a run happens, and a run only ever produces a **proposal** that waits for
a human.

## What your agency grants

Everything else, and the reasoning is consistent: the write either changes a shared
definition or causes something to happen outside the platform.

| Scope | Why it's held back |
|-------|-------------------|
| `metrics:write` | A metric definition is shared. Changing it changes the number everyone sees. |
| `glossary:write` | Same reason — a definition everyone reads. |
| `reports:write` | Reports are what your whole team reads. |
| `alerts:write` | An alert fires notifications at real people. |
| `task_proposals:write` | Approving a proposal sends the Slack message or discussion reply for real. |
| `constraints:write` | A stored benchmark silently changes what every future funnel diagnosis says. |
| `skills:write`, `prompts:write` | They steer how agents behave on your account. |
| BigQuery and PostHog writes | They touch your warehouse and analytics directly. |

<Callout kind="info">
  Nothing here is a permanent restriction. Ask your agency and they can add any of these
  to your key. The split just means those permissions come with a conversation rather
  than a checkbox.
</Callout>

## Permissions your key can never hold

A small set of capabilities belong to your agency's own keys, not to a client key, and
they can't be granted to you at all:

- **Managing clients** — creating, renaming, or archiving client workspaces.
- **The bug-report inbox** — triaging what agents file across all of your agency's clients.
- **Posting to Slack** — sending arbitrary messages. Alerts and approved proposals still
  reach Slack; what's restricted is free-form posting.
- **Running geo experiments** — creating, computing, or deleting one changes live Meta ad
  targeting. You can read every result; your agency runs the experiment.
- **Publishing reports** — you can author and save drafts, and your agency publishes.

These tools aren't in this documentation, because your key will never see them.
