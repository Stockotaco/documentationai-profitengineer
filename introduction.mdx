## What this is

Profit Engineer runs on Mythic OS. It turns your marketing and revenue data into
governed metrics, reports, and alerts — and it exposes all of that to you directly, so
you are not limited to what a dashboard happens to show.

These docs cover that programmatic surface. There are two ways in, and they reach the
same system:

## The one thing worth reading first

**Metrics are the source of truth.** A metric marked *governed* carries its own query,
so resolving it returns the same number for everyone who asks. That is the point of
having them: two people asking the same question should not get two answers.

Anything that recomputes a figure with its own SQL has produced an ad-hoc number, not
your metric. Both surfaces are built to resolve metrics rather than re-derive them, and
it is worth insisting on that from anything you build.
